Comparession Operator

To compare two numbers, we need to use ==
To compare two strings, we need to use .equals("string")
To compare two numbers to determine which is greater or less, we need to use > and <


Logical Operator

To compare two strings, we need to use && and ||
ex(true and true = true, true&&false=false)
ex(true||false = true, true|| true= true)
Here && means and
|| means or
